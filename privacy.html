Privacy Policy — Nacha Studio Publisher

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Nacha Studio Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Nacha Cha TV channels. It is used only by that owner.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google API Services): the app uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish schedules on the owner's own YouTube channels, and reads basic channel and video information and analytics for those channels.
  • Facebook (Meta Platform): the app publishes and schedules videos on Facebook Pages managed by the owner, and reads basic Page information needed to do so.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and password vault. The app does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at Google account permissions or in Facebook Settings > Business integrations. To request deletion of any stored data, email first.thongchai@gmail.com; stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.
